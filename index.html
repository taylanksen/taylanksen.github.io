About me

Education. Research. Real-world impact.

I’m Taylan Sen, a professor at Niagara University with a growing interest in artificial intelligence and facial expressions. I’m fascinated by the ways AI can help us explore patterns in human communication — and by the questions that emerge when technology meets real people.

“The future of AI should be shaped by curiosity, responsibility, and a deeper understanding of people.”

This site is a home for my academic interests, teaching, and a few things I enjoy beyond the screen.
